# Lakshya Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 24, 2026_

Lakshya is a notes, calendar, and reminders app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Data storage

Lakshya stores your notes, events, and reminders locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data stays on your device unless you explicitly enable sync.

## Sync (optional)

If sync is enabled and configured, your data is stored in a Supabase project you (the developer) control, used solely to keep your data available across your own devices. It is not shared with any third party and is not used for advertising.

## Notifications

Lakshya schedules local notifications on your device for reminders and events you create. These are delivered by iOS directly and never pass through any server.

## Optional message reminders

If you choose to enable a message reminder on a specific item, the phone number and message text you enter are stored locally with that item, used only to pre-fill a WhatsApp or Messages compose screen at the reminder's scheduled time. Lakshya never sends this message automatically or transmits this data anywhere — you review and send it yourself.

## Third-party services

- **WhatsApp**: if you use the WhatsApp reminder action, you are redirected to WhatsApp (Meta) or its `wa.me` web fallback, governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
- **Apple Messages**: if you use the Messages reminder action, the standard Apple Messages compose screen is used, governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's developer.
